Security

Found a vulnerability? Tell us.

We build security tools, so we take reports about Fortis seriously. If you act in good faith under this policy, we won't pursue legal action against you.

Email security@fortis.dev
Contents How to reportScopeOut of scopeRules of engagementSafe harbourWhat to expectRecognition

How to report

Email security@fortis.dev with:

  • what you found and where (URL, package or component);
  • steps to reproduce, and a proof of concept if you have one;
  • the impact you think it has;
  • how you'd like to be credited, if at all.

Please don't include customer data in your report. If you came across any, tell us what kind, and delete it.

Scope

  • The Fortis web app and API (app.fortis.dev, api.fortis.dev)
  • Evidence verification links (verify.fortis.dev)
  • The Fortis Guard CLI and its published npm packages
  • This website

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service, load or spam testing
  • Social engineering or phishing of Fortis staff or customers
  • Physical attacks on our offices or providers
  • Customer systems tested through Fortis; report those to the customer
  • Findings from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact
  • Missing headers or best practices with no clear security impact

Rules of engagement

  • Use only accounts you created, and never access other customers' data.
  • Stop and report as soon as you confirm a vulnerability; don't go further than needed to show it.
  • Don't degrade the service for others.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before telling anyone else.

Safe harbour

If you follow this policy, we'll consider your research authorised, we won't take legal action against you, and if a third party does, we'll make it known that you acted under this policy.

What to expect

StepTarget time
Acknowledge your report2 business days
Initial assessment and severity5 business days
Fix for critical issuesAs fast as possible, aiming for 7 days
Fix for other issuesWithin 90 days

We'll keep you updated and tell you when it's fixed.

Recognition

With your permission, we'll thank you publicly once the issue is fixed. A paid bug bounty will follow after general availability.

A machine-readable version of our contact details is at /.well-known/security.txt.
