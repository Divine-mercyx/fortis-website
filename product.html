01 · Pre-push

Fortis Guard. Your first line, on the laptop.

A dev dependency that runs on every push and in CI. It blocks the push when it finds a critical issue, and prints the file, the line and the fix, so the developer never has to leave the terminal.

  • Blocks on criticals, warns on the rest. You set the threshold per project.
  • Scans only what changed before a push, and the full repo in CI.
  • fortis explain shows why it matters and how to fix it, with a code example.
  • Works offline. Rules run locally; only masked finding details are sent to your dashboard.

02 · Fintech rule pack

Rules written for money-moving code.

Generic scanners find generic bugs. Fortis knows what a Paystack key looks like, what a transfer route should check, and where BVNs shouldn't be.

CRITICALFX-SEC-001

Live payment-provider keys in code

Paystack, Flutterwave and Monnify secret keys, live or test, committed to the repo or config.

sk_live_••••••••3f9a
CRITICALFX-OWN-001

Transfer or wallet read without an ownership check

Routes that load a transfer, wallet or account by ID without scoping it to the signed-in user.

findById(req.params.id)
CRITICALFX-HOOK-001

Webhook without a signature check

Payment webhooks that credit wallets without verifying the provider's signature header.

x-paystack-signature
HIGHFX-TXN-001

Balance update outside a transaction

Debits and credits that read and write balances in separate steps, open to race conditions.

balance = balance - amount
HIGHFX-PII-002

BVN or NIN exposed

Bank Verification Numbers or National Identification Numbers in logs, errors or API responses.

logger.info({ bvn })
HIGHFX-IDEM-001

Transfer without an idempotency key

Money-out endpoints that can be replayed and pay out twice on a retry.

POST /v1/transfers
HIGHFX-OTP-001

Weak or leaked OTPs

OTPs logged, returned in responses, generated with Math.random, or with no attempt limit.

Math.random() * 1e6
VARIESDEP-OSV

Vulnerable dependencies

Known vulnerabilities in npm packages, from public advisory data, ranked by whether your code uses them.

package-lock.json
Fix, not just find

Every rule ships with the fix.

Findings come with a short explanation, the risk in plain language, and a code change your developer can copy. Re-run the check and the finding closes itself, with the fix recorded as evidence.

03 · Staging checks

Check every deploy as a real user would.

Some flaws only show up when the app is running. Fortis signs in as your test users and tries to reach what they shouldn't, on staging, after every deploy.

  1. Verify your staging domainAdd a DNS record so Fortis can prove you own the target. No verification, no checks.
  2. Add two test usersUser A and user B, with test data only. Fortis tries to reach B's data as A.
  3. Share your API specOpenAPI or a Postman collection, so every route is covered.
  4. Gate your releasesEach staging deploy runs the checks. Fail the gate on critical findings, if you choose.

04 · Evidence pack

Proof that builds itself.

Every finding, fix and re-test is recorded with a timestamp. Fortis turns that trail into a pack your pentester, partner banks and auditors can start from.

  • Mapped to control areas in the CBN cybersecurity framework, the NDPA 2023 and PCI DSS 4.0.
  • Fix history, not just a snapshot. When each issue was found, fixed and re-tested.
  • Verifiable share links that expire, so a reviewer can confirm the pack is genuine.
  • PDF and CSV export for your audit file.

The evidence pack supports your reviews. It does not replace required third-party tests.
