Company

Making security part of every release in African fintech.

Africa's fintechs move billions every day and ship new code every week. Their security testing still happens once a year. We're building Fortis to close that gap, from Lagos.

We believe the safest release is the one that was checked before it shipped, by the person who wrote it.

Security in fintech is still treated as an event: a consultant arrives, tests for two weeks, and leaves a long report. Meanwhile, teams release every day. The code the pentester tested in March isn't the code customers use in November.

The answer isn't fewer pentests. It's making every release a little safer, automatically, and keeping the proof. That's what lets developers move fast, security leads sleep, and compliance teams answer reviewers with evidence instead of promises.

Fortis starts with Nigerian fintech because the rules, the payment providers and the risks are specific, and global tools don't know them. Then Kenya, Ghana and South Africa.

What we believe

Four principles behind the product.

01

Fix, don't just find

A finding without a fix is homework. Every Fortis rule ships with the change that resolves it.

02

Meet developers where they work

The terminal and CI, not another portal. Security that slows teams down gets switched off.

03

Be honest about limits

Tools don't replace human testers. We say what Fortis doesn't do as clearly as what it does.

04

Safe by default

We hold your most sensitive data. Code stays with you and production is never attacked.
